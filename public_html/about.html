About Hope of Living

Hope of Living is a community-focused organisation based in Lower Philippi, Cape Town. The organisation aims to support people in the community and create opportunities for positive change.

We believe that everyone deserves hope, dignity and support. Through our activities and community projects, we work towards making a positive difference in the lives of individuals and families.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a stronger community where people support one another and have access to opportunities that can improve their lives.

Our Values

Compassion

We care about the needs and experiences of people in our community.

Respect

We treat every person with dignity, kindness and respect.

Community

We believe that working together can create positive change.
